Local knowledge

Built for houses built a century ago.

Oak Park's housing stock has a short list of predictable problems. Knowing them ahead of time is the difference between a two-hour visit and a three-day guess.

Clay lateral

Tree roots in the sewer line

The parkway elms and maples that make the village beautiful send roots straight into the joints of old clay pipe. We rod it, camera it, and tell you honestly whether it's an annual maintenance line or one that needs replacing.

Galvanized

Brown water, weak shower

Supply pipe from the 1920s rusts shut from the inside. Second-floor pressure drops first. Repiping doesn't have to be all at once — we'll show you which runs are worth doing now.

Steam & hot water

Radiators that bang or run cold

A well-tuned steam system still heats a drafty foursquare better than anything. Usually it's traps, vents or pitch — not the boiler. We check the cheap things first.

Combined sewer

Basements that back up in storms

When the village main surcharges, it comes up through your floor drain. Overhead sewers, backflow valves and a properly sized ejector pit are the fix — and we'll tell you which one you actually need.
